# Analysis of Covid-19 Papers
### Management and Analysis of Physics Datasets, Module B
### Physics of Data, Università degli Studi di Padova

COVID-19 Open Research Dataset (CORD-19) is a resource of over 1,000,000 articles about COVID-19, SARS-CoV-2, and related coronaviruses. The project consists of four tasks related to this dataset:
1. Implement a distributed algorithm to count the occurrences of all words within the list of documents.
2. Identify the countries that have been the most and least active in this research field, based on the country of affiliation of the authors. Same for the affiliation institute of the authors to identify the universities and research centers that are cited the most/the least.
3. Obtain embeddings for paper titles.
4. Compute cosine similarity to identify some of the most similar (and most dissimilar) papers in the dataset.
